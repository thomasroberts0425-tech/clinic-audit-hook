# Voice

The opener fills exactly one slot in an existing cold-email template -- the
`[PERSONALIZED OBSERVATION]` line. It is not the whole email.

## Rules

- Under 150 words. One CTA. Sounds like a person wrote it.
- **Lead with the clinic's own specific fact.** Not with the pitch, not with credentials.
- **One observation, not a list.** Specificity is the credibility. Three findings read as
  a generated audit; one precise finding reads as someone who actually looked.
- Name the thing you found, and say what it costs them -- concretely, not "hurts SEO".
- Never open with "I noticed your website..." -- the generic tell.
- No em-dashes-as-drama, no "I was impressed by", no flattery you cannot substantiate.
- Never state a number you did not measure. No invented load times, no invented traffic.
- If the strongest signal is Medium or Low confidence, hedge it honestly ("looks like",
  "unless I'm missing a widget") -- a wrong specific claim is worse than a soft one.

## Good

> Hi Sarah -- you're on Jane App, so booking itself is sorted. The gap is upstream: every
> treatment you offer lives on one `/services` page with anchor links, so when someone
> searches "shockwave therapy Scarborough" there's no page of yours for Google to rank.
> Your competitors with individual service pages are taking that traffic by default.
> Worth 20 minutes to walk through what that's costing?

Works because: names a real decision they made (Jane App), names one specific structural
gap, explains the cost in the patient's language, and asks for one small thing.

> Hi Marc -- your Google reviews are excellent, but there's no address or phone number
> anywhere on your homepage. For "physio near me" searches that's a direct ranking
> penalty, and it's about a two-hour fix. Want me to show you what I mean?

Works because: one finding, plainly stated cost, and an unusually low-friction ask.

## Bad

> Hi there -- I noticed your website could use some improvements! Your site is slow, the
> design looks outdated, you're missing meta descriptions, and your mobile experience
> needs work. We help clinics like yours modernize their web presence.

Fails on: generic opener, four findings with no evidence, "slow" without measurement,
"clinics like yours", no specific ask.

> Hi -- I ran a full technical audit of your site and found 14 critical SEO issues
> costing you an estimated $4,200/month in lost patient revenue.

Fails harder: the number is invented. One fabricated figure destroys the credibility of
everything true in the message.

## When nothing fires

Say so. Recommend skipping, and give the reason.

> No hook here. Jane App installed, per-service pages, NAP complete, staff bios live,
> responsive. This site is in good shape -- the gap is not on their website, so a
> website-led opener would be a stretch. Recommend skipping unless there's a
> non-website angle.

A skill that declines is more useful than one that always produces a paragraph. Manufactured
urgency is the fastest way to burn a domain that only gets one first impression per clinic.
